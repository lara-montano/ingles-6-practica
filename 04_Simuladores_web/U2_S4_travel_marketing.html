    👩‍🏫 For the teacher — how this page maps to the manual
    • Explore is Structure 4 in four objects: the two word lists of the manual as a searchable bank with meanings, examples and the register note (rough it, red-eye, couchsurf informal, promo informal); an ad decoder built on the persuasion techniques the unit identifies (magic words, fact-shaped numbers, scarcity, inviting imperatives, superlatives, evaluative adjectives); a tap-to-match collocation board; the special-cases card and the manual's pitfall list.
    • Build is productive: students assemble the expression a situation needs (manual exercise A and the definitions of the marketing list), then transform — replacing underlined words with a travel expression and completing the marketing report cloze, with validation.
    • Drill merges the quizzes into one graded engine (★ meaning and odd one out · ★★ fix the fixed phrase, complete the term · ★★★ two-word terms, propaganda, persuasion techniques) with a score and streak.
    • Works offline. Reading/writing only — no audio.
