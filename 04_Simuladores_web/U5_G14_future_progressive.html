🕒 Then or during?

Future simple vs. future progressive

will + base names a complete action or a decision at a point (then); will be + -ing paints an action in progress around that point (during). The third box is the form you would use for right now. (Simple = en ese momento, completa; progresivo = durante, en curso.)

will + base · simple
I will call you at eight.
a complete action or a decision · then
will be + -ing · progressive
At eight I will be having dinner.
an action in progress around a future moment · during
am / is / are + -ing · present continuous
She is living the dream right now.
an action in progress right now (for a fixed arrangement, see Grammar 10)
📍 Time markers that suit the future progressive: this time tomorrow · at 8 tonight · in an hour · this time next year · all day tomorrow.
