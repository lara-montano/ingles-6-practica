🧭 Mindset words

The two mindsets — and the words around them

The manual contrasts one pair directly; the rest are the nouns and adjectives you need to describe effort. (Ojo con la forma: sustantivo o adjetivo.)

🌱 a growth mindset

believing ability can be developed — effort and persistence change what you can do.

🧊 a fixed mindset

believing ability can’t change — you either have the aptitude or you don’t.

🗣️ Register. Dream big, put your mind to it, break it down and get in the way are everyday, spoken expressions — perfect for speaking and informal writing. Aptitude, persistence, ambition and procrastinate sound more formal and fit an essay or a job application. (Las frases verbales son coloquiales; los sustantivos abstractos, formales.)

    • Explore is Structure 12 as a deck: the eight verb phrases of the manual grouped by function (set the goal · keep going · get past a setback), a tap-to-match between phrase and situation, the mindset word list with the growth/fixed contrast and a register note, the collocation card (goals · obstacles · motivation · put your mind to it · separable turn down) and the pitfall list.
