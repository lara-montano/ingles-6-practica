💡 How it works

From reality to wish: the same “one step back”

Wish and if only express that reality is not the way we want it. Like the unreal conditionals, they push the verb one tense back to signal “this isn’t true”. Pick a situation and watch reality turn into a wish. (El verbo retrocede un tiempo para marcar que no es real.)

    ⚠ Typical mistakes of Spanish speakers

    Eight traps — and the fix

    The same slips appear in every exam: the verb did not step back, would was used about yourself, or hope replaced wish. (Revisa el tiempo del verbo antes de entregar.)

    • I wish I know the answer. / I wish I have more time. → I wish I knew the answer. / I wish I had more time.one step back: present → past simple
    • I wish I would have studied. → I wish I had studied.past regret = past perfect, no would
    • I wish I would be taller. → I wish I were taller.wish… would is never about yourself
    • I wish to be taller. (meaning regret) → I wish I were taller.wish + to = want (formal), not regret
    • I hope I studied more last year. → I wish I had studied more last year.past regret, not hope
    • It’s time we go. / It’s time that we go. → It’s time we went.it’s time + past simple
    • I’d rather you don’t smoke here. → I’d rather you didn’t smoke here.would rather + someone + past simple
    • I’d rather to go home. → I’d rather go home.same subject: base form, no to
