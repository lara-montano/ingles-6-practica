💡 How it works

Six prefixes that change meaning from the front

A prefix goes before the base word and changes its meaning without changing its class: cook is still a verb in overcook. Tap a prefix and read what it does. (El prefijo va delante y cambia el significado; la clase de palabra no cambia.)

    👩‍🏫 For the teacher — how this page maps to the manual
    • Explore is Structure 13 in two devices: the dial (the six prefixes of the manual's table with their meaning and word lists) and the machine (base + prefix, with the meaning change and a counter of the combinations that do not exist — the manual's dislock / unagree point). The compare card covers un- vs dis-, mis- vs un-, over- vs under-, re- vs un-/dis- and the im- trap; the points-to-watch card is the manual's exception box; the errors card is its pitfall box.
    • Build is productive: students assemble words by choosing the prefix for a meaning (exercise A), then transform — typing the prefixed verb a sentence needs, the informal phrasal twin, or the corrected spelling (exercises B, C and D).
    • Drill merges the quizzes into one graded engine of 13 items (★ meaning of the prefix & real vs invented word · ★★ spelling and the right prefix · ★★★ im-, tense, and the redo/undo pair) with score, streak and medals.
    • Works offline. Reading/writing only — no audio.
