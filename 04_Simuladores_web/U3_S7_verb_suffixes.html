✏️ Spelling

What changes when the suffix arrives

Most bases keep their shape, but some lose a letter or two — and one is irregular. (Cambios de ortografía al formar el verbo.)

🚫 Verbs that do not exist

Do not use the adjective as a verb

Spanish keeps the suffix (modernizar, simplificar), so the English verb needs one too. Without it, the word is still an adjective. (El adjetivo pelón no es verbo.)

-ise is not an error, it is British. organize / organise, realize / realise: both are correct, but pick one spelling and be consistent. This course uses American -ize.
