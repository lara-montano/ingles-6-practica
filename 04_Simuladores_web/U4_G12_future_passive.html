💡 How it works

Shift the spotlight — into the future

You met the passive in Grammar 4 (be + past participle) and predictions in Grammar 10 (will, going to). The future passive joins them: put be into the future — will be + past participle — to say what will be made, worn and sold when the doer is unknown, obvious or unimportant. (Pasiva de futuro: will + be + participio; el agente se omite si es obvio o no importa.)
