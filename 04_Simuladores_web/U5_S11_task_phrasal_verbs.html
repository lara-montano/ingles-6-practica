💡 How it works

Verb + particle = a new meaning

A phrasal verb is an ordinary verb plus one or two small words. The particle changes the meaning completely: put is not put off. Tap a task verb and watch the machine take it apart. (El verbo solo no basta: la partícula cambia el significado.)
