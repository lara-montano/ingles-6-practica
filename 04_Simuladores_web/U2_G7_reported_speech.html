💡 How it works

From “quote” to report: the backshift

When we report words later, the moment of speaking is now in the past, so the verb “shifts one tense back”. Pronouns and time/place words also move. Pick an example and see what changes. (El verbo retrocede un tiempo; también cambian pronombres y palabras de tiempo/lugar.)

    👩‍🏫 For the teacher — how this page maps to the manual
    • Explore shows Grammar 7's backshift on statements, questions and commands, colour-coding every change (reporting verb, verb backshift, pronoun, time/place). The dial is the tense-shift rule; the special-cases card covers say/tell, time & place words, "no shift when still true", and modals.
    • Build is productive: students assemble a report by choosing the backshifted verb, then transform full statements, questions and commands (exercises A–C), with validation.
    • Drill merges the quizzes into one graded engine (★ say/tell & word order · ★★ fix the error/backshift · ★★★ report questions & commands, still-true, must) with a score and streak.
    • Works offline. Reading/writing only — no audio.
