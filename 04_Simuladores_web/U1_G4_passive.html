💡 How it works

Shift the spotlight: active → passive

In the active voice the subject does the action; in the passive it receives it. Form: the right tense of be + the past participle; the doer, if useful, follows by. (La ciencia usa la pasiva cuando importa la acción, no quién la hizo.)

  1. The object of the active sentence becomes the new subject.

  3. Add by + doer only if it is useful.
