💡 How it works

Two families of adjectives

Adverbs like very, completely, highly make adjectives stronger — but they are not interchangeable. English first splits adjectives in two: gradable (more or less is possible) and extreme / absolute (all or nothing). (Antes de elegir el adverbio, decide a qué familia pertenece el adjetivo.)
