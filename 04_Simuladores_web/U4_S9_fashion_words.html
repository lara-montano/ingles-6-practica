⭐ Traps to know

Plurals, two meanings & the verbs that go with clothes

🚫 Common mistakes

What Spanish speakers usually get wrong

a jean → a pair of jeansTwo-part garments are plural. (En español «un jean»; en inglés, un par.)
These clothes is nice. → These clothes are nice.clothes is always plural, so the verb is plural.
This dress fits me. (meaning “it looks good”) → This dress suits me.fit = the right size · suit = looks good on someone. (quedar de talla vs quedar bien.)
very fashion → very fashionablefashion is the noun; the adjective is fashionable.
