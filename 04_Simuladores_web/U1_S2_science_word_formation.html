✍️ Spelling watch

What changes when the suffix arrives

-ic adjective + -allyscientific → scientifically · basic → basically · genetic → genetically. (nunca -icly)
final -e dropsinnovate → innovation · observe → observation · create → creation.
final -y becomes -i-curious → curiosity, as the manual’s Spelling watch lists it: the -ous ending gives way to -osity and the -i- is kept.
doubled consonant (extra)Not in this section of the manual, but worth knowing: plan → planner, control → controlled.

⭐ Word-formation traps

Five places where careful students still fall

⚠️ Typical mistakes — Spanish speakers

The five the manual marks

  • scientifical → scientificEl adjetivo termina en -ic, no en -ical, en esta familia.
  • scientifics say… → scientists say…La persona es scientist (-ist); scientific es adjetivo y no se pluraliza.
  • a big discover → a great discoverydiscover es verbo; el sustantivo es discovery.
  • benefical → beneficialNo se pierde la sílaba -fi-.
  • The genetics are complex. → Genetics is complex.Los sustantivos en -ics son singulares y no llevan artículo aquí.
