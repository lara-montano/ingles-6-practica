💡 How it works

Read the evidence, set your certainty

When we don’t know what happened, we deduce from the evidence. The modal shows how sure we are; the form is always modal + have + past participle. Pick a case and slide the thermometer. (El modal marca la certeza; la forma no cambia.)

👩‍🏫 For the teacher — how this page maps to the manual
  • Explore is Grammar 5's certainty thermometer: the modal marks how sure the speaker is (must have ≈95% yes → can't have ≈95% no). The special-cases card covers could have's two jobs, should have (not deduction), and the continuous & passive forms.
  • Build is productive: students assemble a deduction by choosing the modal that fits the certainty, then transform situations into deductions (exercise C), with validation.
  • Drill merges the quizzes into one graded engine (★ how-certain/choose · ★★ fix the error/complete · ★★★ could-have, continuous & passive) with a score and streak.
  • Works offline. Reading/writing only — no audio.
